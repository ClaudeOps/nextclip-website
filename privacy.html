Privacy Policy

Your videos stay yours. Your privacy is non-negotiable.

✓ TL;DR - The Good News

• Zero data collection. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your data.

• No analytics. We don't track how you use the app.

• No crash reporting. Even crash data stays on your Mac.

• No accounts. No login, no registration, no email required.

• No cloud sync. Everything stays on your Mac.

• No third parties. We don't use any third-party SDKs or services.

Last updated: December 20, 2024

Our Philosophy

NextClip was built on a simple principle: your videos are your business, not ours. We designed the app to be completely privacy-respecting from the ground up.

This isn't marketing speak. NextClip genuinely doesn't collect, transmit, or store any data about you or your videos. This privacy policy exists to document that commitment in clear terms.

What We DON'T Collect

Personal Information

We don't collect your name, email, location, or any other personal information. There's no account system, no login, no registration. You download the app and use it. That's it.

Usage Analytics

We don't track which features you use, how often you use the app, which videos you watch, or how you organize your files. No analytics services, no tracking pixels, nothing.

Video Information

We never see your video filenames, folder structures, file paths, or any metadata. Your video collection is scanned and processed entirely on your Mac. Nothing is sent to our servers because we don't have servers.

Crash Reports

If NextClip crashes (we hope it doesn't!), no crash report is sent to us. macOS may collect crash data locally, but we don't receive it. We rely on user reports to identify and fix issues.

Device Information

We don't collect information about your Mac model, macOS version, hardware specs, or anything else about your device.

What Gets Stored (Locally on Your Mac)

NextClip stores certain information on your Mac to remember your preferences and settings. This data never leaves your computer.

Settings & Preferences

Stored locally using macOS UserDefaults:

  • Your three destination folder paths (for the 1, 2, 3 organization feature)
  • Playback preferences (seek times, playback end behavior)
  • Interface preferences (keyboard hints visibility)
  • Window position and size
  • Last opened folder path

Location: ~/Library/Preferences/com.yourcompany.NextClip.plist

That's it. No hidden files, no background processes, no telemetry. You can verify this yourself by monitoring NextClip's network activity - you'll find there isn't any.

Network Activity

NextClip makes ZERO network requests.

The app doesn't connect to the internet. Not for updates, not for analytics, not for crash reporting, not for anything. It's a completely offline application.

Third-Party Services

We don't use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms. No:

  • Google Analytics
  • Mixpanel
  • Amplitude
  • Sentry
  • Crashlytics
  • Firebase
  • Or any other third-party service

NextClip is built entirely with native macOS frameworks. The only code that runs is code we wrote.

Don't Take Our Word For It

You can verify NextClip's privacy claims yourself:

Network Monitoring

Use tools like Little Snitch or Wireshark to monitor NextClip's network activity. You won't find any outgoing connections.

File System Monitoring

Use tools like fswatch or OpenSnoop to monitor file system access. NextClip only accesses your video files and its preference file.

Code Inspection

As a native macOS app, you can inspect NextClip's binary to verify there are no analytics or tracking libraries included.

Privacy isn't just a promise - it's verifiable.
