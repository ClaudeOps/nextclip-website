Frequently Asked Questions

How many files can NextClip handle?

We've tested with 10,000+ videos. The limit is your hard drive, not NextClip. Our asynchronous architecture prevents UI freezes even with massive collections.

Do I need to learn keyboard shortcuts?

Keyboard hints are displayed on every button (optional setting). Learn as you go. We also provide a complete keyboard shortcuts reference window (⌘/).

What's the "three-key organization" feature?

Set three destination folders in settings. Press 1, 2, or 3 while watching to instantly move the current video to that folder. Perfect for sorting footage or organizing collections.

Why isn't NextClip on the App Store?

Direct distribution lets us deploy fixes faster and host multiple version without the restrictions of the App Store.

What video formats are supported?

NextClip uses macOS native video playback, supporting MP4, MOV, M4V, and 3GP files. If QuickTime can play it, NextClip can play it.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. NextClip has zero tracking, zero analytics, and zero data collection. Everything stays on your Mac. Read our privacy policy.
